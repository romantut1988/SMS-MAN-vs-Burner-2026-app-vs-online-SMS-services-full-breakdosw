### Are free SMS numbers reliable?

Usually not for important accounts. Public numbers can be reused by many users, inboxes may be visible to others, and platforms can reject numbers with a history of heavy verification activity.

It can be risky. If you lose access to the number, you may not receive future 2FA or recovery messages. For important accounts, a persistent number is generally a better choice.

### Does a paid SMS service guarantee verification?

No. Paying for a number does not guarantee that a third-party platform will accept it. Verification systems can reject numbers based on their own security and anti-abuse rules.

### What should I check before buying a number?

Check the supported service, country, number type, price, duration, refund policy, and whether the number can receive future messages. These details are more useful than comparing providers only by their lowest starting price.

Not exactly. Burner is primarily a secondary-number service, while SMS-Activate focuses on temporary SMS verification. If you need ongoing calls, messages, or access to a secondary number, Burner is the more relevant category. If you only need a verification code, a temporary SMS service is usually a closer match.
